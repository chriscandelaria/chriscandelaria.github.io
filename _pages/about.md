Welcome to my website!

I am a Senior Researcher at Basis Policy Research, where I specialize in school finance, using quasi-experimental methods to help policymakers and education leaders understand how resources are allocated and whether those choices expand opportunities for students. I also bring these methods to studies across Basis’s portfolio, from educator compensation and school choice to workforce development.

My research looks at the effects of school finance reforms, educator compensation, and staffing decisions, and it examines how these effects vary across states, districts, and schools. I currently lead Basis’s work to design, develop, and implement a new weighted student funding formula in North Carolina, and I have advised policymakers in Tennessee and Alabama on school finance policy and reform. My work has been funded by the National Science Foundation, the National Institutes of Health, the William T. Grant Foundation, and the Gates Foundation. It has been published in *Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis*, *American Educational Research Journal*, *Education Finance and Policy*, and *Economics of Education Review*, and featured in *The New York Times* and state and local news outlets.

Before joining Basis, I was Research Director at Think*Tennessee*, a nonpartisan think tank, where I led applied research on education policy from early childhood through postsecondary education. Earlier, I was an Assistant Professor of Public Policy and Education at Vanderbilt University’s Peabody College, where I also directed the Master of Public Policy program. I began my career as an economic research associate at the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco. I serve on the advisory boards of EdFund, which connects school finance research with state policymakers, and the SPEND Initiative, which supports national school spending data.

I hold a PhD in Education with concentrations in the economics of education and education policy, an MA in Economics, and a BA in Economics with a minor in mathematics, all from Stanford University.

Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my family, especially exploring Tennessee together. I also find peace of mind in music: playing piano, composing, and singing in a choir.
